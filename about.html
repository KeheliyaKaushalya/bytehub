Our Story

ByteHub was founded in 2015 with a simple mission: to provide high-quality computer hardware and accessories at competitive prices. What started as a small shop in Colombo has grown into one of Sri Lanka's leading technology retailers.

We believe that everyone deserves access to the best technology. Whether you're a professional, a gamer, a student, or a business owner, we have the right products to meet your needs. Our team of experts is always ready to help you find the perfect solution.
